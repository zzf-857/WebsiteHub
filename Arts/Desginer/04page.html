Extendable launcher for Mac

Raycast is a blazingly fast, totally extendable launcher. It lets you complete tasks, calculate, share common links, and much more. It's designed to be a better replacement for macOS Spotlight, putting everything you need just a few keystrokes away.

How it works

1

Summon the Bar

Use a global hotkey (Option + Space) to bring up the minimal, high-speed command bar anywhere on your desktop.

2

Type Your Intent

Search for apps, files, or specific commands like "Convert Currency" or "Join Next Meeting".

3

Instant Action

Execute immediately. No mouse needed. Everything is keyboard-optimized for maximum velocity.
